My Favourite Motto:


"Job's not finished"


This was said by, basketballer, Kobe Bryant during a press conference on June 7, 2009. He was asked why he isnt smiling after taking the lead in the NBA finals. He then responded "Job's not finished." This shows just how relentless he was. refusing to celebrate until the task was completed.
